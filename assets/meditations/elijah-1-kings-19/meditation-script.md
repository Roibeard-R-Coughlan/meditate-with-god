# After the Fire

### A meditation on 1 Kings 19

[Music only. Allow the listener to arrive.]

Take a slow breath in.

And then gently release.

Let your shoulders soften.

For these next few moments, you do not need to solve anything.

You do not need to arrive here strong.

Just be here.

With God.

[REFLECTIVE SILENCE]

Elijah had just witnessed something extraordinary.

On Mount Carmel, he had stood before the people of Israel and the prophets of Baal.

He had prayed.

And fire had fallen.

But almost immediately afterwards, everything changed.

Queen Jezebel sent word that she intended to kill him.

And Elijah became afraid.

He ran.

He left his servant behind.

Then he walked alone for another day into the wilderness.

Until eventually…

he sat beneath a tree.

And something in him gave way.

“ENOUGH!”

[PAUSE]

Elijah asked God to take his life.

The prophet who had stood so boldly before hundreds…

had nothing left.

No great prayer.

No courageous speech.

No plan.

He lay down beneath the tree…

and slept.

[LONG REFLECTIVE SILENCE]

And notice what God does.

He does not begin by rebuking Elijah.

He does not immediately tell him to become stronger.

An angel touches him.

“Arise and eat.”

There is bread.

There is water.

Elijah eats…

and lies down again.

[PAUSE]

Then the angel returns a second time.

“Arise and eat…

because the journey is too great for you.”

[LONG REFLECTIVE SILENCE]

Let those words settle.

The journey is too great for you.

God does not pretend Elijah is stronger than he is.

He meets him where he is.

He gives him what he needs…

before asking him to continue.

[LONG REFLECTIVE SILENCE]

Perhaps you know something of that feeling.

A season that has taken more from you than you expected.

A responsibility that has worn you down.

A prayer that seems to have gone unanswered.

A moment when all you could think was…

Enough.

[PAUSE]

You do not have to hide that from God.

Elijah didn’t.

And God did not abandon him there.

[LONG REFLECTIVE SILENCE]

Strengthened by what he had received, Elijah travelled on.

For forty days and forty nights…

until he reached Horeb.

The mountain of God.

There he entered a cave.

And God asked him:

“What are you doing here, Elijah?”

[PAUSE]

Elijah poured out what was inside him.

His disappointment.

His fear.

His loneliness.

He believed he was the only one left.

And then God told him to stand on the mountain.

A great wind came.

Strong enough to tear at the rocks.

But God was not in the wind.

Then the earth shook.

But God was not in the earthquake.

Then came fire.

But God was not in the fire.

And after the fire…

a gentle whisper.

[VERY LONG REFLECTIVE SILENCE]

Elijah heard it.

And he covered his face.

The man who had seen fire fall from heaven…

stepped forward for the whisper.

[LONG REFLECTIVE SILENCE]

What if God’s quietness does not mean His absence?

[LONG SILENCE]

What if you have been waiting for God to become louder…

while He has been inviting you to become quieter?

[VERY LONG REFLECTIVE SILENCE]

Perhaps there is something you have been carrying before God.

You do not need to force an answer now.

You do not need to manufacture a feeling.

Just become still enough to listen.

[LONG REFLECTIVE SILENCE]

God asked Elijah again:

“What are you doing here?”

[REFLECTIVE SILENCE]

Perhaps you can allow that question to reach you too.

Not as an accusation.

As an invitation.

What has brought you here?

What are you carrying?

What are you afraid of?

What have you become tired of trying to hold together?

[VERY LONG REFLECTIVE SILENCE]

Elijah believed he was alone.

He believed his story had reached its end.

But from inside that cave…

he could not yet see the whole story.

God still had people Elijah did not know about.

God still had work for him to do.

God still had a road ahead.

[PAUSE]

And Elijah did go back.

He learned that he was not the only faithful one left.

And soon, he met Elisha…

someone who would walk with him and eventually carry the work forward.

The man who entered the cave believing he was alone…

would not walk the next part of the journey alone.

[LONG REFLECTIVE SILENCE]

The cave was not the end.

The exhaustion was not the end.

Even his cry of “Enough!” was not the end.

[LONG REFLECTIVE SILENCE]

Maybe you do not need to see the whole road tonight.

Maybe you only need enough grace for the next step.

Enough strength for today.

Enough quietness…

to hear the whisper.

[VERY LONG REFLECTIVE SILENCE]

God…

meet me where I am tired.

Meet me where I am afraid.

Meet me where I feel alone.

Give me what I need for the journey ahead.

And when I expect You to speak through something dramatic or unmistakable…

teach me to recognise You in the quiet.

[LONG SILENCE]

For these final moments…

do not search for another thought.

Do not search for another answer.

Remain here.

Quietly.

With God.

[LONG SILENCE — music continues]

After the fire…

came the whisper.
